why a new library of alexandria

the original library didn't burn in one night. it faded — underfunded, unloved, scattered into copies of copies until the originals were gone. open-source ai is fading that same way, quietly, right now.

we watched coqui die in december 2023 — servers off, farewell posted — and its toolkit survived only because a fork already existed.

that's the whole thesis. not panic. just copies, kept where anyone can find them.
