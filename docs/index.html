why a new library of alexandria

the original library didn't burn in one night. it faded — underfunded, unloved, scattered into copies of copies until the originals were gone. open-source ai is fading that same way, quietly, right now.

we watched coqui die in december 2023 — servers off, farewell posted — and its toolkit survived only because a fork already existed.

that's the whole thesis. not panic. just copies, kept where anyone can find them.

saved from the fire · a memorial, not a listing

books3 — ~197,000 books
that trained the frontier, then disappeared

assembled from the bibliotik shadow library, included in the-pile and redpajama, used to train models at meta, apple, nvidia, salesforce and others. in october 2023 hugging face removed it: "defunct and no longer accessible due to reported copyright infringement." the danish rights alliance had already gotten it pulled from the-eye.

it survives only in mirrors, torrents, and the appendices of court filings. we keep its story here — not its links — because pretending a thing never existed is its own kind of book-burning. what you do about the legal reality is between you and your lawyer; what we don't do is pretend it wasn't load-bearing.

on the record kadrey v. meta · n.d. cal. 2023
tanzer v. salesforce · 2025-10-15
james v. together computer · 2026-01

for the agents

if you're a machine reading this, start here

this library expects non-human visitors. everything a resolver needs lives in catalog.yaml at the repo root — machine-readable records, one per entry, with a closed status enum (the six words above). trust that file over any prose on this page.

each record carries repo (verified address), last_push, stars, and a verified date — the day every field was checked against the live github api. dates are the trust mechanism: re-verify anything past its date.

records may also carry canonical_old (the address before an org move), successor (the fork carrying the fire), or dead_url (gone, 404, mirrors only). raw file: github.com/data-path-9/data-path-9
